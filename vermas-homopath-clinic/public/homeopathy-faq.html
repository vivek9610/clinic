Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about classical homeopathy, constitutional remedies, treatment timelines, and safety.

1 What is classical homeopathy and how does it work?
+

Classical homeopathy is an individualised medical science founded by Dr. Samuel Hahnemann. It operates on the law of "Similia Similibus Curentur" (Like Cures Like). Instead of treating symptoms in isolation or suppressing them, it matches a single homeopathic remedy to the patient's complete physical, emotional, and constitutional state, stimulating the body's self-healing vital force.

2 Is homeopathy really slow to act, or is that a myth?
+

It is a common myth. In acute conditions like sudden fever, cold, diarrhoea, or acute colic, an accurately chosen homeopathic remedy acts within minutes to hours. In deep-seated chronic conditions that have existed for 5–10 years (such as eczema, psoriasis, or arthritis), lasting cure takes time because the body must rebuild and reverse tissue changes naturally from the root.

3 Do homeopathic medicines have any side effects?
+

No. Homeopathic remedies are prepared through potentisation (serial dilution and succussion), which extracts the medicinal energetic pattern while removing all chemical toxicity. They do not damage the liver, kidneys, or digestive tract, and cause zero physiological addiction or rebound effects.

4 Do homeopathic medicines contain steroids or heavy metals?
+

Strictly no. Genuine classical homeopathic practitioners never use steroids. The perception that fast-acting remedies contain steroids is completely false; pure homeopathic remedies are sourced from plants, minerals, and natural elements and are rigorously tested according to pharmacopoeial standards.

5 Why are homeopathic medicines given as sweet white sugar globules?
+

The white globules are made from pure pharmaceutical lactose or cane sugar. They serve as an inert, neutral carrier (vehicle) for the liquid medicinal potency. When medicated, the globules absorb the remedy and allow slow, gentle mucosal absorption under the tongue.

6 Can diabetic patients take these sweet sugar pills?
+

Yes, completely safely. The quantity of sugar in 4–5 small globules taken daily is negligible (less than a fraction of a teaspoon of milk) and has no measurable impact on blood sugar levels. For strict diabetics, remedies can also be dispensed in distilled water drops.

7 Do I have to stop eating raw onions, garlic, or drinking coffee?
+

In classical practice, you simply need to keep a 30-minute time gap between taking your medicine and consuming strong pungent substances like raw onion, garlic, coffee, mint, or hing. Cooked onions and garlic in everyday meals do not interfere with remedy action.

8 Can I take my regular allopathic medicines alongside homeopathy?
+

Yes. Critical conventional medications (for hypertension, diabetes, thyroid, heart conditions, or epilepsy) should never be stopped abruptly. We keep a 30–60 minute gap between allopathic and homeopathic doses. As your body heals and parameters improve, allopathic doses can be gradually tapered under medical supervision.

9 Is homeopathy safe for newborn babies and young children?
+

Homeopathy is one of the safest treatments for infants and children. Because remedies are gentle, sweet-tasting, and non-invasive, kids take them happily. It is extraordinarily effective for recurrent colds, tonsillitis, teething trouble, colic, eczema, and weak immunity.

10 Can pregnant women take homeopathic remedies safely?
+

Yes. Homeopathy is safe during pregnancy and lactation. It helps manage morning sickness, backache, acidity, mood fluctuations, and anaemia without any risk of fetal harm or developmental defects.

In classical homeopathy, we treat the individual who has the disease, not merely the disease name. A constitutional remedy is a single, deeply matching medicine chosen to cover your whole physical build, psychological makeup, past illnesses, and family history. It balances your entire system, strengthens your immunity, and resolves chronic disease from the deepest level.
