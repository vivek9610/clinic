Diet & Lifestyle Guide

Homeopathic treatment works from within — but diet and lifestyle choices either support or slow down that process. This guidance is general, not a substitute for what we tell you personally at your consultation.

Universal Lifestyle Tips

Applicable to all patients during homeopathic treatment, regardless of condition. Follow these evidence-based daily habits to maximize remedy effectiveness and support your body's natural vital force.

Hydration

  • 2.5–3 litres of water daily, spread evenly through the day.
  • Warm water on an empty stomach in the morning to stimulate digestion.
  • Avoid very cold / iced water with meals — it can slow down your digestion.
  • Fresh coconut water or jeera (cumin) water are beneficial additions.
  • Limit carbonated soft drinks, artificial sodas, and sugary canned drinks.

Meal Timing

  • Don't skip breakfast — eat a nutritious meal within an hour of waking.
  • Try to eat dinner before 8 PM wherever possible.
  • Leave a comfortable 3–4 hour gap between your main meals.
  • Avoid heavy, oily, or spicy meals right before going to sleep.
  • Chew your food slowly and mindfully — it genuinely helps gut motility and nutrient absorption.

Medicine Timing

  • Take homeopathic medicine 30 minutes before or 30 minutes after food.
  • Avoid strong coffee or tea around the time you take your medicine.
  • Let the sweet globules dissolve under the tongue — do not chew or swallow them whole.
  • Store medicines away from direct heat, sunlight, camphor, and strong perfumes.
  • Take medicine at roughly the same time each day — therapeutic consistency matters.

Sleep

  • Aim for 7–8 hours of sound sleep — this is genuinely part of the treatment, not optional.
  • Try to be asleep before midnight to match natural circadian rhythm.
  • Avoid mobile and TV screens for at least 30 minutes before bed.
  • Keep the bedroom quiet, dark, well-ventilated, and cool.
  • Keep a consistent wake-up time every morning, even on weekends.

Movement

  • At least 30 minutes of brisk or moderate walking every single day.
  • Practice yoga or pranayama for 15–20 minutes, 4 times a week if possible.
  • Avoid sitting for long unbroken stretches — stand up or stretch every hour.
  • Moderate exercise is completely fine during treatment unless specifically advised otherwise.
  • Avoid extreme overexertion or exhausting workouts during acute disease flare-ups.

Stress Management

  • Chronic stress is one of the biggest factors that slows down treatment response.
  • Anulom-vilom or simple diaphragmatic breathing practice, 10 minutes daily, helps tremendously.
  • Tell us about any major emotional shock or distress during treatment — they are deeply relevant to your case.
  • A short daily walk in fresh air and greenery is a simple, proven stress-reliever.
